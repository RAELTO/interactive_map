My Neighborhood

My neighborhood is a quiet and friendly place with many useful locations. There is a supermarket near Maple Avenue and a pharmacy near Central Avenue. The library and the hospital are south of Pine Street, while the park is east of Lake Avenue. There is also a school, a bank, a café, a gym, a police station, and a store. I like my neighborhood because most places are close to my home and easy to reach on foot.
